A private surf lesson just for your group

Blue Barrel Surf School runs private group surf lessons in Cornwall for stag and hen parties, large groups, birthdays and corporate events. We never mix groups, so it's just your group, at your pace, with an ISA-qualified instructor.

If you're looking for a group activity in Cornwall that everyone can join, surfing is hard to beat. Nobody needs experience, and the laughs start with the first wipeout. The only requirement is being able to swim 25 metres unaided.

Group surf lessons for every occasion

One of the most original group activities in Cornwall. Whether it's a milestone birthday, friends on a weekend away or a family celebration, we make it memorable, safe and tailored to your group's energy and ability.

Group surf lesson questions

How many people can join a group surf lesson?

We run private group surf lessons for up to 16 people. Groups of up to 8 can book online. For 9 to 16 people, get in touch and we'll send a quote.

How much do group surf lessons cost?

A private 2-hour lesson is £100 for the first person and £30 for each extra person, up to 8. For example, 6 people pay £250 in total (about £42 each) and 8 people pay £310 (about £39 each). Groups of 9 to 16 are quoted on request.

How far in advance should we book a group session?

We recommend at least 2 to 4 weeks for groups of 9 or more, and as early as possible for summer stag and hen bookings, because peak weekends fill up fast. Get in touch as soon as you have a date in mind.

Can you run stag surf lessons in Newquay?

Yes. We regularly run stag surf lessons in Newquay at South Fistral, one of the UK's most iconic surf beaches and a brilliant backdrop for a stag do.

What if our group has mixed abilities?

That's completely normal and we're well set up for it. We assess everyone at the start and adapt the coaching, so complete beginners and people who've surfed before can be in the same session.

Do we need any experience or equipment?

None at all. No surfing experience is needed and we provide everything: wetsuits, surfboards, boots and gloves. The only requirement is being able to swim 25 metres unaided.

Where do group surf lessons take place?

Across Cornwall, including South Fistral in Newquay, Gwithian and Porthkidney in St Ives Bay, Perran Sands, Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands. We choose the best beach for your group on the day.
