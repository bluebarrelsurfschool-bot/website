Meet Jack, founder and head surf instructor

Jack is an ISA Level 1 surf instructor and RLSS-qualified lifeguard, with over 12 years of surfing and eight years of professional coaching. Before starting Blue Barrel, he coached at surf schools across Cornwall: group lessons, private lessons, school sessions, surfing and paddleboarding.

He grew up surfing on the Isle of Wight, largely without guidance. There was no plan and nobody to explain why he wasn't progressing. Blue Barrel is built to give you what he was missing: structure, goals to aim for and support along the way.

Whether you're catching your first wave or working on your first bottom turn, Jack is in the water with you, not coaching from the beach.

Private and mobile, by design

Blue Barrel only teaches private lessons. Your session is never shared with strangers, and the coaching is built around where you are now and where you want to get to.

Being mobile means we're not tied to one beach. Cornwall's north and south coasts face different directions, so when one side is too big or blown out, the other is often clean. Every lesson goes to the beach that best suits the conditions on the day. See all our surf lesson locations in Cornwall.

Jack's favourite? Gwithian in St Ives Bay, for its three miles of sand, Godrevy lighthouse and the quiet spots you can find even in peak summer.

About Blue Barrel: questions

Are your surf instructors qualified?

Yes. Jack, our founder and head instructor, is an ISA Level 1 qualified surf instructor and RLSS qualified lifeguard, with over 12 years of surfing experience and 8 years of professional coaching at surf schools across Cornwall.

Where is Blue Barrel Surf School based?

We're a mobile surf school, so we're not tied to one beach. We teach across Cornwall, including South Fistral in Newquay, Gwithian and Porthkidney in St Ives Bay, Perran Sands, Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands.

What surfboards do you use?

We carry a full range of boards, from wide, stable learner boards to mid-lengths and hybrids for improvers, so you're always on the right board for your level.

Are your lessons really private?

Yes. We never mix groups. Your lesson is just you or the people you book with, and coaching is built around your level and goals.
