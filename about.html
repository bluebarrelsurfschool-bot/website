Why I started a private, mobile surf school

I started Blue Barrel because private lessons should be private. No groups of eight strangers. No shouted instructions from the shore. Just you, the ocean and coaching built around where you are right now and where you want to get to.

Being mobile means I'm never tied to one beach. Cornwall's north and south coasts face different directions, so when one side is too big or blown out, the other is often clean and surfable. Every session happens at the beach best suited to the conditions on the day. See all our surf lesson locations in Cornwall.

How I coach

  1. 1

    The right beach for your level

    I check the swell, wind and tide every morning and choose the safest, most suitable beach, from South Fistral in Newquay to Gwithian in St Ives Bay and Praa Sands on the south coast.

  2. 2

    The right board for you

    I carry a full range of surfboards, from wide, stable learner boards to mid-lengths and hybrids for improvers, so you're always on the right equipment for your level.

  3. 3

    In the water with you

    Coaching happens next to you in the waves, not from the beach. You get instant, honest feedback and a clear idea of what to work on next.

About Blue Barrel: questions

Are your surf instructors qualified?

Yes. Jack, our founder and head instructor, is an ISA Level 1 qualified surf instructor and RLSS qualified lifeguard, with over 12 years of surfing experience and 8 years of professional coaching.

Is Blue Barrel Surf School insured?

Yes. Blue Barrel Surf School is fully insured for every lesson we run.

Where is Blue Barrel Surf School based?

We're a mobile surf school, so we're not tied to one beach. We teach across Cornwall, including South Fistral in Newquay, Gwithian and Porthkidney in St Ives Bay, Perran Sands, Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands.

What surfboards do you use?

We carry a full range of boards, from wide, stable learner boards to mid-lengths and hybrids for improvers, so you're always on the right board for your level.

Are your lessons really private?

Yes. We never mix groups. Your lesson is just you or the people you book with, and coaching is built around your level and goals.
