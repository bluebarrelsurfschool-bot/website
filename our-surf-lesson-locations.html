One mobile surf school, seven Cornish beaches

Most surf schools are tied to one beach, so you get whatever that beach serves up on the day. Blue Barrel is a mobile surf school. We bring the boards, wetsuits and an ISA-qualified instructor to whichever beach has the best waves for your level.

Cornwall has two coastlines facing different directions. When the wind or swell spoils one side, the other is often clean and surfable. Working across both coasts means fewer cancelled lessons and better waves, whether it's your first time on a board or you're learning to ride green waves.

Surf lessons on Cornwall's north coast

The north coast faces the open Atlantic and picks up the most consistent swell in Cornwall. It's where we surf most of the year, especially in summer.

South Fistral, Newquay

Surf lessons in Newquay · Best winds: easterly and south-easterly

Fistral is Newquay's best-known surf beach and one of the most famous breaks in the UK. We teach at the southern end, which is often a little quieter than the main beach. Its sandbanks produce consistent, well-shaped waves for beginners in the white water and for improvers ready to try green waves.

Gwithian, St Ives Bay near Hayle

Surf lessons near Hayle and St Ives · Best winds: easterly to southerly

Gwithian sits in the long, sandy sweep of St Ives Bay, with Godrevy Lighthouse at its northern end. There's plenty of space to spread out and long runs of gentle white water, which makes it one of our favourite beaches for beginner surf lessons and family surf lessons.

Porthkidney Sands, St Ives Bay near Carbis Bay

Surf lessons near St Ives, Lelant and Carbis Bay · Best winds: southerly to westerly

Porthkidney is a wide, quiet beach between Lelant and Carbis Bay. It faces a different direction to most of the north coast, so it's sheltered when south-westerly and westerly winds blow the other beaches out. When a big swell makes the open beaches too powerful for learners, Porthkidney is often small, clean and just right.

Perran Sands, Perranporth

Surf lessons in Perranporth · Best winds: easterly and south-easterly

Perran Sands is a long stretch of sand north of Perranporth, backed by dunes. The beach is big enough to find an uncrowded peak even in the summer holidays, and the waves roll in steadily over sandbanks, which suits beginners and improvers alike.

Surf lessons on Cornwall's south coast

The south coast needs a bigger swell to wake up. When it does, it's often clean while northerly winds blow the north coast out, and it's a favourite in autumn and winter storms.

Marazion, near Penzance

Surf lessons near Penzance · Best winds: northerly and north-easterly

Marazion faces St Michael's Mount across Mount's Bay, making it one of the most scenic places in Cornwall to learn to surf. It comes to life in bigger south-westerly swells and gives gentle, rolling waves that are ideal for first-timers.

Perranuthnoe, Mount's Bay

Surf lessons near Marazion and Penzance · Best winds: northerly and north-easterly

Perranuthnoe is a small, sandy village beach a short drive east of Marazion. It picks up south-westerly swell, and at the right stage of the tide it's a great spot for improvers to practise catching waves in a quieter setting.

Praa Sands, between Penzance and Helston

Surf lessons near Helston and Porthleven · Best winds: northerly and north-westerly

Praa Sands is a long, south-facing beach and one of the most popular surf beaches on the south coast. When a south-westerly swell arrives and the wind is from the north, it offers clean, fun waves for every level, from first-timers to confident intermediates.

Surf lesson location questions

Where do your surf lessons take place in Cornwall?

We teach on seven beaches: South Fistral in Newquay, Gwithian and Porthkidney Sands in St Ives Bay, and Perran Sands at Perranporth on the north coast, plus Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands in Mount's Bay on the south coast.

Do you run surf lessons in Newquay?

Yes. We teach at South Fistral, the southern end of Newquay's Fistral Beach, when the conditions suit your level.

Do you run surf lessons near St Ives and Hayle?

Yes. We teach at Gwithian near Hayle and at Porthkidney Sands between Lelant and Carbis Bay, both in St Ives Bay.

Are there surf lessons near Penzance?

Yes. On the south coast we teach at Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands, all in Mount's Bay and within a short drive of Penzance and Helston.

Which Cornish beach is best for beginner surf lessons?

It depends on the day. Long, sandy beaches with gentle white water, such as Gwithian, Perran Sands and Praa Sands, are often ideal, but the right choice changes with the swell and wind. We pick the safest beach for your level each morning.

Can I choose which beach my lesson is at?

Yes, you can choose a beach when you book. If the conditions there aren't safe or suitable on the day, we'll suggest a better beach nearby.

What happens if there are no waves, or the waves are too big?

Because we're mobile, we move your lesson to whichever beach has the right conditions for your ability. If every location is genuinely unsuitable, we'll work with you to reschedule.
