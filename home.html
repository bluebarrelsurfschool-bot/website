Surf lessons in Cornwall: your questions

How much is a surf lesson in Cornwall?

A private 2-hour surf lesson is £100 for one person, and each extra surfer adds £30, up to 8 people. All equipment is included.

Where do your surf lessons take place?

We're a mobile surf school, teaching at South Fistral in Newquay, Gwithian and Porthkidney in St Ives Bay, Perran Sands, Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands. We choose the best beach for your level on the day.

Are your surf lessons suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. We coach complete beginners, improvers and intermediates. Most first-timers stand up in their first session.

Can children have surf lessons?

Yes, from age 5, as long as a parent or guardian is in the water with them throughout.

What do I need to bring?

Just your swimwear and a towel. We provide the wetsuit, surfboard and all accessories.
