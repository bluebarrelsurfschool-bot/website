Blue Barrel Surf School booking terms

1. Booking and payment

  1. 1.1

    All lessons must be booked in advance through our website.

  2. 1.2

    Full payment is required to confirm your booking.

  3. 1.3

    We only accept payments via credit or debit card.

2. Cancellation and refund policy

  1. 2.1

    Cancellations made at least 48 hours before the scheduled lesson will receive a full refund.

  2. 2.2

    Cancellations made within 48 hours of the lesson are not eligible for a refund, as this reflects the short notice and difficulty in re-filling spaces. At our discretion, we may offer a reschedule or credit note.

  3. 2.3

    If Blue Barrel Surf School cancels a lesson due to adverse weather conditions or unforeseen circumstances, we will offer a full refund, reschedule, or a voucher.

3. Participant requirements

  1. 3.1

    Participants must be capable of swimming at least 25 metres unaided.

  2. 3.2

    Any medical conditions, allergies, or physical limitations must be disclosed before participation at the booking stage.

  3. 3.3

    The parent or guardian retains primary responsibility for the minor’s safety and participation. Blue Barrel Surf School provides instruction and takes reasonable care but does not replace parental supervision.

  5. 3.5

    Bookings for under-18 participants will only be accepted if the parent or guardian confirms they will remain present and responsible for the entire session.

Booking and cancellation questions

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made at least 48 hours before the scheduled lesson receive a full refund. Cancellations made within 48 hours of the lesson are not eligible for a refund, although at our discretion we may offer a reschedule or credit note.

What happens if you cancel my lesson because of the weather?

If Blue Barrel Surf School cancels a lesson due to adverse weather conditions or unforeseen circumstances, we will offer a full refund, a reschedule or a voucher.

Do I need to be able to swim to have a surf lesson?

Yes. Participants must be able to swim at least 25 metres unaided.

Can children and under-18s take part?

Yes. Anyone under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, who must stay present and in the water for the whole session. We teach children from age 5.

How do I pay for a surf lesson?

Lessons are booked in advance through our website, and full payment by credit or debit card is needed to confirm your booking.
