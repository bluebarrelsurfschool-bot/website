Gift voucher questions

How do I buy a surf lesson gift voucher?

Email us a gift voucher request at bluebarrelsurfschool@gmail.com, telling us which lesson you'd like to give and how many people it's for. We'll send you a payment link, and once you've paid you'll receive a digital gift voucher with a unique code.

How long is a surf lesson gift voucher valid?

Gift vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.

How does the recipient redeem their gift voucher?

Every digital gift voucher has a unique code. The recipient uses it to redeem their surf lesson and book a date and time that works for them.

What does a surf lesson gift voucher include?

A 2-hour private surf lesson with an ISA-qualified instructor, with all equipment included. Lessons are tailored to the surfer's goals and experience, from complete beginners to improvers.

How much is a surf lesson gift voucher?

A private 2-hour surf lesson is £100 for one person, and each extra surfer adds £30, up to 8 people. So a voucher for two is £130, and for a family of four it's £190.

Can children use a surf lesson gift voucher?

Yes, from age 5, as long as a parent or guardian is in the water with them throughout the lesson.
