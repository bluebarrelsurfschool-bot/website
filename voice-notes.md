# Jack's own words (for rewriting the site in his voice)

Collected from the owner, lightly tidied. Use these as the source for page copy; keep his phrasing and first person.

## 1. Why did you start Blue Barrel?
Raw: "I started Blue Barrel Surf School after years of coaching across Cornwall, teaching group lessons, private lessons, school lessons, coaching paddleboarding and surfing. I wanted to create a business and a surf school that I wish I had access to when I was growing up and when I was learning to surf. I wish I had the kind of structure that Blue Barrel can offer, giving you goals to aim for, support along the way."

Draft for the site: "I started Blue Barrel after years of coaching across Cornwall: group lessons, private lessons, school sessions, surfing and paddleboarding. I wanted to build the surf school I wish I'd had when I was learning: one with real structure, goals to aim for, and someone in your corner along the way."

Open follow-ups: how many years coaching; where he grew up / learnt; what was missing when he learnt.
