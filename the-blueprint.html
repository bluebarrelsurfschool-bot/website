Most surfers plateau. The Blueprint fixes that.

You get in the water. You catch waves. You feel like you're improving. Then six months later you realise you're doing the same things, in exactly the same way.

The problem isn't effort, it's direction. Without a clear structure, a coach who knows where you're going and real feedback after every session, you're just practising the same habits on repeat.

By the end of the Blueprint, you'll surf differently.

Not marginally better. Differently. You'll understand why you catch or miss waves, what your body is doing wrong and how to fix it, and what to work on every time you paddle out, with or without a coach.

How the Blueprint coaching system works

Every Blueprint follows the same five-part coaching structure, whichever track you're on. Each private session builds on the last.

  1. 1

    Assessment

    Your first session starts with a diagnostic look at your surfing: how you paddle, pop up, position yourself and read waves. We find the habits holding you back.

  2. 2

    Personal progression plan

    After the first session you get a written progression plan built around your ability and goals, so every session after that has a clear purpose.

  3. 3

    Targeted correction

    Coaching in the water, next to you, fixing one thing at a time. Drills and pressure tests make sure new technique holds up in real waves.

  4. 4

    Video analysis after every session

    Footage of your surfing, sent via WhatsApp with written session notes and your focus points, so you can see exactly what's changing.

  5. 5

    Handover

    A full written handover document at the end: an independent practice plan, your focus areas and your next goals, so you keep progressing without a coach.

Two tracks, one system

The Blueprint is designed for two types of surfer. Both tracks follow the same coaching structure. The destination is different.

Beginner track

From first pop-up to green waves

Who it's for

Surfers who are brand new, or who have had a lesson or two but can't consistently get to their feet. You may have surfed before, but without the proper technique.

Where you'll be by the end

Paddling into green waves, getting to your feet with correct technique, reading basic wave patterns and surfing independently and with confidence.

The journey

  1. Ocean safety
  2. Consistent pop-up
  3. Whitewater control
  4. Green wave entry
  5. Independent surfing
Intermediate track

From stalled progress to surfing with intent

Who it's for

Surfers riding green waves, but inconsistently. You make some waves, miss others and don't fully understand why. Progress has stalled, and turns are accidental instead of deliberate.

Where you'll be by the end

Surfing with intent. Understanding every wave you ride and every turn you make. Real technique, not survival instincts.

The journey

  1. Diagnostic assessment
  2. Core technique fix
  3. Wave reading
  4. Speed and trim
  5. Deliberate turning
  6. Independent performance

The Blueprint: your questions

Who is the Blueprint surf coaching programme for?

The Blueprint has two tracks. The beginner track is for surfers who are brand new, or who have had a lesson or two but can't consistently get to their feet. The intermediate track is for surfers riding green waves inconsistently, whose progress has stalled.

How much does the Blueprint cost?

Blueprint 3 is £375 for three 2-hour private coaching sessions. Blueprint 6 is £750 for six 2-hour private coaching sessions. Both include video analysis after every session, a progression plan and a handover document.

What's the difference between Blueprint 3 and Blueprint 6?

Blueprint 3 is the full programme in condensed form: assessment, correction, breakthrough, pressure tests and handover. Blueprint 6 extends each phase with more drilling and consolidation, and adds two extra layers: deliberate turning and performance surfing.

How do I get my video analysis?

Video analysis is sent via WhatsApp after every session, with written session notes and your personal focus points. You also get WhatsApp and email support between sessions for the whole programme.

What happens when the Blueprint finishes?

You receive a full written handover document with an independent practice plan, focus areas and next goals. You can also continue into a second Blueprint at the returning client rate.

Where do Blueprint sessions take place?

We're a mobile surf school, so each session happens at the Cornish beach that best suits your level and the conditions, including South Fistral in Newquay, Gwithian and Porthkidney in St Ives Bay, Perran Sands, Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands.
