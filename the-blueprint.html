Most surfers plateau. The Blueprint fixes that.

You get in the water. You catch waves. You feel like you're improving. Then six months later you realise you're doing the same old habits, in exactly the same way.

The problem isn't effort, it's direction. Without a clear structure, a coach who knows where you're going and real feedback after every session, you're just practising the same habits on repeat.

The promise

By the end of the Blueprint, you'll surf differently.

Not marginally better. Differently. You'll understand why you catch or miss waves, what your body is doing wrong and how to fix it, and what to work on every single time you paddle out, with or without a coach.

Two tracks, one system

The Blueprint is designed for two types of surfer. Both tracks follow the same coaching structure: assessment, targeted correction, progression plan, video analysis and handover. The destination is different.

Beginner track

From first pop-up to green waves

Who it's for

Surfers who are brand new, or who have had a lesson or two but can't consistently get to their feet. You may have surfed before, but without the proper technique.

Where you'll be by the end

Paddling into green waves, getting to your feet with correct technique, reading basic wave patterns and surfing independently and with confidence.

The journey

  1. Ocean safety
  2. Consistent pop-up
  3. Whitewater control
  4. Green wave entry
  5. Independent surfing
Intermediate track

From stalled progress to surfing with intent

Who it's for

Surfers riding green waves, but inconsistently. You make some waves, miss others and don't fully understand why. Progress has stalled, and turns are accidental instead of deliberate.

Where you'll be by the end

Surfing with intent. Understanding every wave you ride and every turn you make. Real technique, not survival instincts.

The journey

  1. Diagnostic assessment
  2. Core technique fix
  3. Wave reading
  4. Speed and trim
  5. Deliberate turning
  6. Independent performance

Every session has a purpose

Blueprint 6 is the full programme: every milestone is hit, with more application, more consolidation and two additional layers, fluid turning and confident surfing. For a more condensed approach, Blueprint 3 covers every core milestone in a shorter format.

  1. 01

    Assessment: find what's holding you back

    Jack observes your natural surfing before saying a word, and video is filmed throughout. This is the diagnostic: everything is built from it.

  2. 02

    Identifying the weakness: drilling the fix

    The single biggest limiting factor is targeted and drilled with real-time feedback. Video comparison from session one shows the change as it happens.

  3. 03

    Breakthrough: technique becomes automatic

    Corrections stop being conscious and start being instinctive. The next technical layer is introduced, and wave reading and positioning come into focus.

  4. 04

    Pressure: pushing your limits

    Technique is pushed into more challenging conditions. What's genuinely locked in becomes clear, and so does what still needs work before the final session.

  5. 05

    Refinement: adding the finishing touches

    A full overview of your technique and competency in the water, leaving no stone unturned.

  6. 06

    Handover: the final session and your surfing plan

    A full transformation video, session one against session six, and a written handover document: your independent practice plan, what to focus on and what to work towards next.

The Blueprint: your questions

Who is the Blueprint surf coaching programme for?

The Blueprint has two tracks. The beginner track is for surfers who are brand new, or who have had a lesson or two but can't consistently get to their feet. The intermediate track is for surfers riding green waves inconsistently, whose progress has stalled.

How much does the Blueprint cost?

Blueprint 3 is £375 for three 2-hour private coaching sessions. Blueprint 6 is £750 for six 2-hour private coaching sessions. Both include video analysis after every session, a progression plan after session one and a written handover document.

What's the difference between Blueprint 3 and Blueprint 6?

Blueprint 6 is the full programme. Every milestone is hit, with more application, more consolidation and two additional layers: fluid turning and confident surfing. Blueprint 3 is the condensed version, covering every core milestone in a shorter format.

How long do I have to use my Blueprint sessions?

Blueprint 3 is valid for 6 months and Blueprint 6 is valid for 12 months.

How do I get my video analysis?

After every session you receive video clips of your surfing via WhatsApp, with a written or voice note breakdown of exactly what to take away, followed by written session notes with your 2 to 3 personal focus points.

Can I ask questions between sessions?

Yes. WhatsApp and email support is available throughout the full programme. Ask questions, share clips and check in on your progress, and Jack responds.

What happens when the Blueprint finishes?

You receive a full written handover document with an independent practice plan, what to focus on and what to work towards next. You can also continue into a second Blueprint at the returning client rate.

Where do Blueprint sessions take place?

We're a mobile surf school, so each session happens at the Cornish beach that best suits your level and the conditions, including South Fistral in Newquay, Gwithian and Porthkidney in St Ives Bay, Perran Sands, Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands.

Just want to try surfing first? Our private 2-hour surf lessons start at £100, with all equipment included.
