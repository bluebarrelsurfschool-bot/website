## Description

Blue Barrel Surf School is a private, mobile surf school in Cornwall, founded by ISA-qualified surf coach and RLSS-trained lifeguard Jack. Every lesson is private: just you, your family or your friends with your instructor, never mixed with strangers. Lessons last two hours and all the kit is included, with winter wetsuits, boots and gloves from November to March. We check the forecast every morning and teach at the beach that suits your level that day, from South Fistral in Newquay and Gwithian in St Ives Bay to Perran Sands, Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands. We coach beginners, improvers and families, with children welcome from age 5 alongside a parent. Lessons run all year round.

## Services

### Private surf lesson (£100)

A 2-hour private surf lesson with an ISA-qualified instructor, all equipment included. £100 for one person, plus £30 for each extra person up to 8. For beginners and improvers, at the Cornish beach that suits your level on the day.

### Family surf lesson (£190 for four)

One private 2-hour lesson for the whole family, all kit included. Children are welcome from age 5 with a parent or guardian in the water, and everyone is coached at their own level.

### Group surf lesson (Price on request)

Private group lessons for stag and hen parties, team days and birthdays, up to 16 people. No experience needed and all equipment included. Up to 8 people from £100 plus £30 each; larger groups priced on request.

### Winter surf lessons (£100)

Private 2-hour lessons from November to March at 10am and 1pm, with a 5/4mm winter wetsuit, boots and gloves included. Quieter beaches and more consistent swell, and we pick a sheltered beach when it's stormy.

### The Blueprint coaching programme (From £375)

A structured private coaching programme of 3 or 6 two-hour sessions for beginners and improvers, with video analysis after every session and a personal progression plan.

### Surf lesson gift voucher (From £100)

A gift voucher for a private 2-hour surf lesson, sent instantly as a PDF by email. Valid for 12 months, so they can pick any date. Vouchers for one, two or a family of four.

Why private surf lessons?

Every Blue Barrel lesson is private: just you, your family or your friends with your instructor. That means the whole two hours goes on what you want to work on, whether that's standing up for the first time, catching green waves or feeling confident out the back.
