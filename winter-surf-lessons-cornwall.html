Why we love surfing in Cornwall in winter

Most people put the board away in October. We don't. Winter is when Cornwall's surf is at its most consistent: Atlantic storms send swell our way week after week, and with the summer crowds gone you'll often have a peak almost to yourselves.

It's a good time to learn, too. Fewer people in the water means more room and more waves each, and in a private lesson your instructor is only watching you.

“In winter we get some huge swells, but the headland protects St Ives Bay from a lot of wind directions, so there's often a gentler wave tucked away even in a big storm.”Jack, founder

Where we surf in winter

Winter swell is bigger and the wind changes more often, so picking the right beach matters more than in summer. Because we're mobile, we go wherever the waves suit your level that day.

Big swell days

St Ives Bay: Gwithian and Porthkidney

St Ives Bay picks up swell all winter, and the headland shelters it from a lot of wind directions. When the open beaches are too big, there's often a smaller, cleaner wave tucked into one corner of the bay. See surf lessons at Gwithian.

South-westerly winds

South Fistral, Newquay

The cliffs at the southern end of Fistral shelter the water from southerly and south-westerly winds, which blow through a lot of Cornish winter storms. That makes South Fistral a reliable winter spot when other beaches are choppy.

When the north coast is too big

Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands

When a big north-westerly swell closes out the north coast, the south coast is often smaller and cleaner. It's a short drive, and it can save a lesson that would otherwise be rescheduled.

Your winter surf lesson, from the car park to a hot drink

  1. 1

    We pick the beach that morning

    Every morning starts the same: forecast, webcams and a mocha. Before your lesson we check the conditions suit your level, then confirm where to meet.

  2. 2

    Kit up in the car park

    We bring your wetsuit, boots and gloves and help you get them on. Come with your swimwear on under warm clothes, so changing is quick.

  3. 3

    Warm up on the sand

    A few minutes of moving and stretching gets the blood flowing. Then we run through safety and the pop-up before getting in.

  4. 4

    Two hours in the water

    Paddling, catching waves and walking back out keeps you warm. Most people are surprised by how warm they stay in a good winter suit.

  5. 5

    Get warm, fast

    Straight out of the wetsuit and into dry layers. A changing robe and a flask of something hot make the walk back to the car much nicer.

What to bring to a winter surf lesson

We provide the wetsuit, boots, gloves and board. You just need the things that keep you warm before and after.

  • Swimwear, worn under your clothes
  • A big towel
  • Changing robe or warm layers
  • Woolly hat
  • Flask of something hot
  • Snacks for afterwards

Winter surf lesson questions

Is it too cold to surf in Cornwall in winter?

No. The sea around Cornwall usually stays between about 10°C and 13°C from November to March. With a 5/4mm winter wetsuit, boots and gloves, most people stay comfortable for the whole lesson, and moving around keeps you warm.

Can complete beginners have a surf lesson in winter?

Yes. We choose a beach with smaller waves that suit your level, and with fewer people in the water there's more room to learn.

What wetsuit do you provide in winter?

A 5/4mm winter wetsuit, plus wetsuit boots and gloves, all included in the price. On the coldest days we add a hood.

What times are winter surf lessons?

From November to March, lessons start at 10am and 1pm. If you need a different time, email or call us and we'll do our best to fit you in.

What happens if there's a storm on the day?

We move to a sheltered beach that suits your level, such as a corner of St Ives Bay or the south coast. If every beach is unsafe, we'll rearrange your lesson for another day.

Can children have surf lessons in winter?

Yes, from age 5, as long as a parent or guardian is in the water with them, just like in summer. Children feel the cold more quickly, so we keep a close eye on how warm they are.

Can I buy a winter surf lesson as a Christmas present?

Yes. Our gift vouchers arrive as a PDF by email straight away and are valid for 12 months, so they can choose a winter or a summer lesson.
