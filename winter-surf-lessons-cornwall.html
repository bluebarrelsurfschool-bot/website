Why surf in Cornwall in winter?

Winter is when Cornwall's surf is at its most consistent. Atlantic weather systems send swell to the coast week after week, and with the summer crowds gone you'll often have a stretch of beach almost to yourselves.

It's a good time to learn, too. Fewer people in the water means more room and more waves each, and in a private lesson your instructor is only watching you. With a proper winter wetsuit, boots and gloves, most people are surprised by how warm they stay.

Where we surf in winter

Winter swell is bigger and the wind changes more often, so picking the right beach matters more than in summer. Because we're mobile, we go wherever the conditions suit your level that day.

When the swell is big

St Ives Bay: Gwithian and Porthkidney

The headland shelters St Ives Bay from a lot of wind directions, so there's often a smaller, cleaner wave in one corner of the bay even in a big storm. See surf lessons at Gwithian.

When the wind is south-westerly

South Fistral, Newquay

The cliffs at the southern end of Fistral shelter the water from southerly and south-westerly winds, which come with a lot of Cornish winter weather.

When the north coast is too big

Marazion, Perranuthnoe and Praa Sands

When a large north-westerly swell closes out the north coast, the south coast is often smaller and cleaner, and only a short drive away.

Winter surf lesson questions

Is it too cold to surf in Cornwall in winter?

No. The sea around Cornwall usually stays between about 10°C and 13°C from November to March. With a 5/4mm winter wetsuit, boots and gloves, most people stay comfortable for the whole lesson, and moving around keeps you warm.

Can complete beginners have a surf lesson in winter?

Yes. We choose a beach with smaller waves that suit your level, and with fewer people in the water there's more room to learn.

What do you provide in winter?

Everything: a 5/4mm winter wetsuit, wetsuit boots, gloves and a surfboard matched to your level, all included in the price. A hood is optional if you'd like one.

What times are winter surf lessons?

From November to March, lessons start at 10am and 1pm. If you need a different time, email or call us and we'll do our best to fit you in.

How long is a winter surf lesson?

Two hours, the same as the rest of the year. If you'd prefer a shorter session, let us know when you book.

What happens if there's a storm?

Winter often brings storms, so we may have to change location if the conditions aren't right for your lesson. We'll always give you as much notice as possible.

Can children have surf lessons in winter?

Yes, from age 5, as long as a parent or guardian is in the water with them. Lessons are normally 2 hours, but we can make them shorter if you ask, which can suit younger children in winter.

Can I buy a winter surf lesson as a gift?

Yes. Our gift vouchers arrive as a PDF by email straight away and are valid for 12 months, so they can choose a winter or a summer lesson.
